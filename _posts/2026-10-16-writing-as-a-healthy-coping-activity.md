I've been in a lot of things in these years, and in the last 3 months I was searching if there's a way to focus when everything goes wrong: if there's something healthy to cope with when I have a lot of things in my head.

I started to get passionate of the concept of finding a way to optimize and improve my brain to focus, when I'm under pressure or distracted, especially when I have a lot of mentally demanding work, like studying or coding.

I found out that when something is not easy to solve or understand, the first thing my brain does is to escape from the task I was trying to finish. Finishing wasting hours of thinking about other parts of my life. 

This behaviour get worse when something in my life happened and I can't do anything. 

So one day, tired of feeling helpless, I found out an old notebook, and I start writing what goes wrong in that moment and how I felt.. and it worked very well! 

I'm doing it right now, writing my experience and thoughts in this blog. 

When I say "writing", I mean every way a person can do to catch thoughts and memories about something and describe it with his own words. 

The way and the things I write in my diary are totally different from my blog in this website, but there are two different way to do the same thing. 

So here some reasons to start!
<hr>

##### **1. Helps to analyze your emotions**

Writing helped me to understand what's going on in my mind. 

Describing my days in these months improved my ability to analize my pattern and learning something useful about myself and what I want. 

<hr style="width: 90%; margin: 15px auto;">

##### **2. Keeps track of your journey**

It's difficult to keep track of our progress, or what we have to do to achieve our goals.. Sometimes we don't even know why we start things or what's our final goal. 

It's really easy feeling lost, especially when we do a lot of things without asking ourself how we feel about that.

Writing helps us to keep in mind what are out motivations and what are our projects in the future.

So keep your diary with you and start writing about what you want to achieve in your next year and then write their related tasks.

<hr style="width: 90%; margin: 15px auto;">

##### **3. It's a good brain exercise**

You can learn a lot of things writing. For example in my case, (if you're not new in my site you'll have already noticed) this is my first article in english.

I will force myself to write english articles in fadda.dev to improve my writing skills, and I hope to be able to make some progress. 

This is an example but there are different ways to learn something from this habit, it depends from what's the goal of what you're writing. 

But in general, writing without any learning goals keeps you're mind active anyways. 

<hr style="width: 90%; margin: 15px auto;">

##### **4. You're thoughts couldn't last forever**

I found fascinating the concept of writing my own thoughts in the paper and the realization that the moment I start writing, my idea could last forever.

There are times where I don't have enough time to keep track of my life, and suddenly I feel like I was missing something about my days. 

This thing is enough for me to keep myself writing when I feel demotivated to continue. 

It's difficult to remember the people we were and how the life changed us, writing helps me to take a picture of the person I was in that moment.

I wanna read 10 years later what I wrote and remember who I was the moment I start.

<hr>

Ok, I think it's enough.

I hope this article will give you the first step to start your writing journey... Have fun!
